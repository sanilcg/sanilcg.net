I bought this domain a long time ago. The account says December 2005.

Over the years it has been a lot of things. A Coppermine photo gallery. A Joomla site. A WordPress blog. A place where I dumped kernel configs, Apache build scripts and half-written notes. Then, for a long while, nothing.

When I logged in again this week, I found what happens to a website nobody looks after. Someone had planted a phishing kit in it: fake login pages for Outlook, Yahoo and AOL, sitting in folders with names like `tmpp2`, quietly waiting for victims. My little corner of the internet had been working for someone else.

So I cleaned house. Deleted twenty years of leftovers, kept a handful of old photos, and rebuilt the whole thing from scratch: plain files, nothing to hack, nothing to forget to update.

This version is meant to be simpler. A place for pieces: things I notice, things I remember, photos that mean something to me. Not polished. Not in order.

Let's see how long I keep it going this time.
